## Ranked leads

A. (best) Tilted Person body vs. tilt compensation. Aim pitch compensates body tilt with `- front.y` only (a sine, pitch only, roll and the gate flags ignored; item 6). With a tilted ejected pilot, shot direction = body tilt (pitch+roll) composed with `0.5*input - sin(pitch)`, while the camera (FP POV bone) is level + pitch input. Errors: pitch residual `p - sin p`, plus roll rotating the muzzle offset (GC1 sits low/right of the lens, skill notes: "gun sits ~0.13 m behind the lens"), which looks like "lower and to the right". Matches "airborne or shortly after landing" (tilt present at spawn). Does NOT by itself explain a constant range-independent offset; the rolled GC1 offset (parallax) would.
B. Animated GC1 pose: muzzle origin is the live GC1 bone (item 4). Eject-phase clips (idleParachute 9 / landParachute 10 / jump 11, FSM in `FUN_0059d340:486-573`) key GC1 differently from crouch, so un-scoped HUD-crosshair shots leave from the clip pose, not the lens. A range-independent lower-right offset is exactly constant muzzle-vs-lens parallax. Not eject-specific on its own unless the sniper pose is not reached (state 0 -> stand2Kneel only when grounded: lines 505-540).
C. Rejected: stale `weapon+0x28` (refreshed every Simulate, item 7); stale FOV/zoom (hit resolution has no zoom term, item 1); scope camera vs fire matrix orientation (same node, item 7).

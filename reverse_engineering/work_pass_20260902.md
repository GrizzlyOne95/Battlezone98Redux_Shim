## Primary Target 1 — Walker cockpit jitter

### Goal
Determine exactly which transform (or inheritance) step causes first-person
cockpit jitter in walkers, distinguishing intentional walker motion from motion
that should not propagate to the first-person cockpit/camera. Do not add
smoothing/interpolation as a first fix — it can conceal the real problem while
introducing latency, aiming errors, or animation divergence.

### 1.3 — Native transform trace (design — evidence standard)

**Target hierarchy (exact field names differ — recover actual architecture, do
not force terminology):**

```

    ↓
Walker world transform             (GameObject world pos/rot)
    ↓
```

### 1.4 — Why tanks/scouts don't jitter (comparison to be driven by trace)

**Proven asset delta:**

- `avwalk.mesh` 28 bones (`AGe11POV`/`age21hed` children of `age11hed`) →
  `avwalk_c.mesh` 26 bones/15 tracks, re-rooted.
- `svwalk.mesh` 15 bones (`svw11POV` child of `svw11hed`, `svw21hed` child of `svwalk`) →
  `svwalk_c.mesh` 16 bones/10 tracks, both cockpit/head re-rooted, roots
  `svwalk00`/`svwalk01` added.
- Submesh vertices: `avwalk 5260/88/153` vs cockpit `4177/88/153`; `svwalk 6912/197`
  vs cockpit `6398/197`.

This alone disproves “generic copy of world parent” and is why a global
inheritance change would be unsafe — it could break animation, aiming,
attachments, or ordinary cockpits.

The required gated comparison: same per-frame matrix set for `avwalk` vs
`avtank` under identical `fwd`/`slope`/`haim`/`movaim` cases. The tank's
`SceneNode → Entity → Camera` chain must be shown stable where the walker's
`cockpit parent → POV bone → FP entity → Camera` diverges.

### 1.5 — Patch policy (not yet eligible)

Only implement if the offending transform component is sufficiently understood.
A desirable fix removes only the component that should not propagate into first
person while retaining: yaw/orientation, aiming, recoil, authored cockpit animation,
intentional positional offsets, world walker animation, external rendering. No
broad frame smoothing unless the engine architecture proves interpolation is the
intended missing operation (it is not proven).

Candidate shape (illustrative, **not a production patch**): if the trace proves
that vertical stride/suspension jitter on `svwalk00`/`avwalk_*c POV` parent
oscillates the FP camera while `world SceneNode.posit_y` is driven by terrain
250 mm per cycle, a narrow hook would stop that bone's Y inheritance into the
`View_Record.MainCam` path, leaving yaw/aim/recoil untouched.

```
        |
```

Implementation discipline (matching `Pilot Carrier Null Guard`/`Neutral Attack`

## Secondary Target 5 — 32-bit resource / virtual-address lifecycle telemetry

### Goal
Instrument mission transitions to determine whether large custom campaigns suffer
cumulative resource retention and/or VA fragmentation. Redux is already Large
Address Aware (2–3 GB user VA); do not “enable LAA”. The question is lifecycle
inside the existing 32-bit process.

### Critical metric

Not just “free RAM” but **largest contiguous free VA block**:

```
600 MB total free but largest contiguous = 70 MB → request 100 MB contiguously → fails.
```

Determine whether that largest-free metric shrinks monotonically across missions.

### Points to capture

At each lifecycle point — `process startup`, `before mission load`,
`after mission load`, `after settle`, `mission end`, `after teardown`,
`before next mission`, `after next mission`, `return to shell` — capture:

- Process: `private bytes`, `working set`, `commit` (via `GetProcessMemoryInfo`).
- VA: `total free user VA`, `largest contiguous free VA` (walk `VirtualQuery`
  from `0x00010000` → `0x7FFEFFFF`/`0xBFFFFFFF` under LAA, tally free runs).
- Ogre: `texture count`, `mesh count`, `skeleton count`, `material count`,
  `Modable resource count` (`ResourceGroupManager::listResourceNames` counts;
  already used by `terrain_proxy.cpp` and `ogre_render_profile.cpp`).
- If trustworthy, `resource size estimates`.

### Harness

- Deterministic multi-mission heavy-content fixture if one exists cleanly (or
  `lcbench` looping with a synthetic `heavy.mat` add). Measure across ≥5
  missions — fragmentation that is harmless for 2 may be fatal at 5.
- Log CSV per point: `mission,capture,label,privateMB,wssMB,largestFreeMB,totalFreeMB,texCount,meshCount,...`
- Plot `largestFree` vs mission index; a downward staircase is fragmentation
  evidence; flat largest-free with rising Ogre counts is leak evidence.

### Patch policy

Telemetry first. Do not call broad Ogre `clearResourceGroup("Modable")` or
`unloadUnreferencedResources` during ordinary mission transitions until
retention is demonstrated and a safe window is understood (`leave_game_freeze_shader_recompile` and `Modable` notes warn of shader-compile stalls on the wrong thread/timing).

### Status

- **Proven:** `va_lifecycle` shape is fully specified, Ogre counts are already
  reachable, and the LAA context is settled.
- **Unknown:** whether fragmentation grows (needs runs).
- **Changes:** none yet — design only.
- **Recommendation:** implement `src/patches/va_lifecycle_telemetry.cpp` as a
  `Diagnostics` opt-in (`VACapture=1`) that hooks `Mission::Load`/`Cleanup` on
  the known lifecycle seam and writes the CSV outside `addon`. Do not start
  retention fixes until at least one heavy-fixture retention curve exists.

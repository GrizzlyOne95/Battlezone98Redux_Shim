[h1]Campaign Reimagined and OpenShim: Roadmap[/h1]

[b]Updated 2026-10-28. Current version: Campaign Reimagined with OpenShim 1.0.0.64 and EXU 1.3.2.[/b]

[b]OpenShim[/b] is a community patch for Battlezone 98 Redux. It fixes engine bugs, adds the in-game OpenShim Options menu and powers the new visuals. [b]Campaign Reimagined (CR)[/b] is the remastered NSDF campaign built on top of it. [b]Extra Utilities (EXU)[/b] gives mod and mission makers the same tools.

[b]Where things stand:[/b] nearly everything on the original plan is done and shipping. The main remaining work is a handful of stubborn [b]multiplayer bugs[/b], listed below.

[h2]Done[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]Stability[/b] - many stock crashes fixed, including crashes on save/load, after winning a mission or restarting after death, game exit, ejecting, satellite view and long Lua messages in multiplayer. The startup freeze some players hit is gone, and the game survives a graphics-driver reset on DirectX 11. When something does crash, OpenShim writes a crash report you can send us.
[*] [b]Saves[/b] - AutoSave with an adjustable interval and optional backups, and AutoSave no longer fires on menus or half-loaded missions.
[*] [b]Gameplay and AI[/b] - Constructor, Recycler, APC, Tug, Howitzer and Minelayer fixes; smarter AI targeting, weapon ranges and scrap gathering; optional Battlezone 1.5 behaviors such as satellite fog of war.
[*] [b]OpenShim Options menu[/b] - every setting in one place, in game: a full keybind editor, raw mouse input, field of view, DirectX 11/DirectX 9 choice, run in background and more. Most fixes can be switched off if you prefer stock behavior.
[*] [b]HUD[/b] - custom HUD and radar colors, the classic scrap/pilot layout, reticle styles, hit markers, incoming-damage arrows, HUD elements that fade when idle, and recolorable menus.
[*] [b]Visuals[/b] - a hand-made sky for every world (Earth over the Moon, Jupiter over Io and Europa, Saturn through Titan's haze), high-detail terrain and painted ground, scrap geysers, physical wreckage chunks, muzzle flashes and recoil, shell casings, better headlights, and a pilot flashlight.
[*] [b]The campaign[/b] - all 17 NSDF missions, Red Arrival to The Ultimate Quake, under [b]Custom Campaign > Campaign Reimagined[/b]. The last few missions are still being finished.
[*] [b]Co-op[/b] - every mission is also a 2-4 player co-op map (CR 01 to CR 17 Coop), with respawns near a teammate and a co-op PDA for pings and rescue requests.
[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b] - map search and filters, persistent mutes and bans, host controls, clear messages when you can't connect, and a networking fix that reduces stutter in busy games.
[*] [b]Install and updates[/b] - one-click setup from inside the game or a standalone installer, and automatic updates from the Workshop.
[*] [b]For modders[/b] - EXU and bzfile add Lua tools for cameras, HUD, materials, AI, production, file saving and more.
[/list]

[h2]Still Open: Multiplayer[/h2]

These are known problems we are still tracking down. Detailed reports and log files help most.

[list]
[*] [b]Daywrecker[/b] - occasional double detonations, and terrain spikes after a hit.
[*] [b]Splinter[/b] - shells that appear twice or collide oddly.
[*] [b]Joining and leaving[/b] - friend invites don't carry the game password; problems when the host leaves; mission win/lose not always reaching every player.
[*] [b]Scoreboard[/b] - some kills credited to the wrong player, especially sniper kills by the host.
[*] [b]Free camera[/b] - players in a match can sometimes use a free camera they shouldn't have.
[*] [b]Bad connections[/b] - long stalls when packet loss is heavy.
[*] [b]Real-world testing[/b] - co-op is tested heavily on one PC; we need more sessions between real PCs, on Steam, and on Linux/Steam Deck (Proton).
[/list]

[h2]Also on the List[/h2]

[list]
[*] Rare crashes on Shift-F9, Alt-Tab, Alt-Enter and fullscreen switches.
[*] AI that gets stuck, or stops building in very long games.
[*] Walker cockpit jitter, and a rare sniper-scope misalignment after ejecting.
[*] Finishing the last campaign missions.
[/list]

[h2]Experiments[/h2]

LAN hosting and a nearby-host browser are in development on a separate branch. They still require Steam/Galaxy sign-in and need testing between real PCs; they are not included in the release.

Not part of the release, and may never be: DLSS frame generation and ray-traced shadows, terrain tessellation, and fog that swirls around passing hovercraft.

[h2]Install and Update[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]Install:[/b] [b]Single Player > Instant Action > ! SETUP / REPAIR - Open Community Patch[/b], then exit the game fully. Or download [url=https://github.com/GrizzlyOne95/Battlezone98Redux_Shim/releases/latest/download/OpenShimSetup.exe][b]OpenShimSetup.exe[/b][/url], which finds your Steam or GOG copy.
[*] [b]Update:[/b] OpenShim installs Workshop updates automatically and offers [b]Restart now[/b]. To check by hand: [b]Options > OpenShim > Check for Updates[/b]. Turn automatic updates off with [b]Auto-Install Updates[/b].
[/list]

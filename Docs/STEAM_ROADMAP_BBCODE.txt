[h1]OpenShim Progress Reference[/h1]

[b]Updated: 2026-10-09. Source audit: 2026-10-07; native UI/network integration: 2026-10-09.[/b]

Canonical progress reference for OpenShim, Extra Utilities (EXU), Campaign Reimagined (CR), bzfile and the community multiplayer server. OpenShim owns native engine fixes and shared settings; EXU owns reusable Lua/runtime APIs; CR and ISDF Chronicles provide consuming content; bzfile owns protected file/update primitives.

[b]Reading status:[/b] Implemented means code exists at the audited revision. Qualification pending identifies a remaining runtime, visual, multiplayer or platform check. Unfinished foundations have code but do not yet deliver the intended feature. Open reports require reproduction or diagnosis and are not automatically confirmed engine defects.

The source audit, pinned revisions and historical evidence are in [code]Docs/ROADMAP_CODE_VALIDATION_20261007.md[/code]. The October 9 combined branch, deployment hashes, GOG menu verification and remaining network gates are in [code]Docs/NETWORK_UI_INTEGRATION_20261009.md[/code]. Current-main APIs and consuming mod source do not establish inclusion in an older bundled DLL. Local GOG results do not qualify Steam, Proton or Wine.

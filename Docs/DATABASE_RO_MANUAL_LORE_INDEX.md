Source and citation key
- **RO pN** = Steam PDF "TheRedOdyssey_Manual.pdf" (13 PDF pages; each is a two-page spread), PDF page N, with printed page numbers in brackets. Text extracted with pdftotext (the Read tool's PDF mode lacked pdftoppm). PDF p5 extraction is column-garbled; readings from it are marked (garbled).

- Compared against `manual_lore_index.md` (existing index; cited as **E**).
- All ODF mappings are UNVERIFIED: the manual states no ODF names. Mappings below are guesses by analogy to E.

### Black Dog support units (RO p8 [12])
- Recyclers, Scavengers and Factories are the same as standard NSDF vehicles except for markings/insignia; the reader is referred to the NSDF Field Briefing Manual.
- Scavenger piloting: scrap pickup/dump uses the K key; pilots may end up temporarily flying Scavengers; turrets and artillery use the same K deploy mechanic (RO p5 [7]).

## 6. Factions
- **Black Dogs (NSDF Black Dog Squadron)**: elite NSDF squadron; manual is their field briefing guide (June 1969, HQ NSA); their kit is -BD variants of standard NSDF vehicles; rear-echelon units identical to normal with different markings (RO p1, p6-8).
- **Chinese (Red Army)**: established on Ganymede, with vehicles built for operations there; offensive capabilities poorly known; radio chatter in Mandarin; gray tanks with a star; suspected cloak tech (RO p4, p9-10).
- **CCA (Soviet)**: in the Jovian system for Fury technology; US suspects Russian involvement in 4 Platoon's loss but has no evidence of CCA forces on Ganymede (RO p3, p5 [7]).
- **NSDF**: US force; Black Dogs are its elite squadron (RO p1).
- Campaign order advice: play Black Dog missions before Red Army missions; Red Army missions suit more competent players; no training missions; access via Single Player then TRO button (RO p11 [18]).

## 7. Characters and story facts
- **4 Platoon**: Black Dog unit on scrap-collection protection (dozen or so Scavengers, pilot plus two wingmen in Grizzlys) on Ganymede for two weeks (RO p3 [3]). Player callsign "Delta 3-0"; wingman "Delta 3-1" (RO p4 [4]).
- Raid: two gray, star-marked small tanks raid the Scavengers; one Scavenger destroyed; they vanish from sight (cloak implied). Pilot assigns one wingman to guard remaining five Scavengers (RO p4 [4]).
- Chinese base found; large tanks appear and destroy the wingman; pilot's Grizzly hit three times and he reaches for the ejector seat (RO p4 [5]).
- Intelligence briefing dated Feb 3, 1970 (garbled), Eyes Only: 4 Platoon lost contact 49 hours ago (RO p5 [6-7]); Russian involvement suspected; patrols dispatched; no evidence of CCA on Ganymede; HQ forces limited in manpower and confined to a small area.
- Prototype program: Red Devil test timeline (RO p8 [13]).
- No named people other than call signs. No Cthonian names beyond the single unnamed reference to "Cthonian technology" (RO p10 [17]).

## 8. Controls and gameplay facts (for completeness)
- K deploys/undeploys turrets and artillery; Scavenger K = scrap collect/dump; Tug K = pick up objects (RO p11 [19]).
- Group units with Ctrl+F1-F7 (RO p11 [19]).
- Recommend Automatic Levelling OFF (RO p11 [18]).
- Multiplayer needs broadband; TRO vehicles usable (RO p12 [20]).

## 9. Conflicts with existing index (E)
1. **Faction name**: E says no Black Dog unit models or "Chinese/Red Odyssey" content exists; TRO supplies 10 BD units and 9 Chinese units. E sec 3's "Black Dogs ... no unit models listed" is now answered: -BD variants (sec 1 above).
2. **Black Dog vs NSDF designations**: all -BD designations equal the E designations plus "-BD" (M580A2, M60A7, M60A8, M173, M47A9, B74CT, AAV6A4, M183A1, AT9). Badger remains **M173** (-BD), supporting E's M173 over the guide's "T7"; "T-4"/"T-7" still unsupported.
3. **Grizzly armament**: E lists standard Grizzly as AT-Stabber, Minigun, Manual Detonation Mortar, Thumper; TRO says BD Grizzly standard is "SP Stabber" and Prox-mines. "SP Stabber" is a new weapon name vs E's "AT-Stabber".
4. **Wolverine armament**: E: Hornet, Shadow, Proximity Mine; TRO BD: Shadow Rocket launcher only. Naming "Shadow Rocket" vs "Shadow Missile".
5. **Razor armament**: E: 2x Minigun; TRO BD: two cannon plus one rocket hardpoint.
6. **Thunderbolt armament**: E: 2x Rocket Bomb (BFE9); TRO BD: TAG Cannon, Sandbag, Comet Rockets. New weapon names (TAG Cannon, Sandbag, Comet Rockets) are not in E.
7. **Bobcat armament**: E manual: AT-Stabber, Hornet, Solar Flare; TRO BD: AT-Stabber only.
8. **Sasquatch armament**: E: 2x AT-Stabber + Pop Gun; TRO BD: twin AT-Stabbers only.
9. **Badger**: both give a minigun; TRO adds BD condition notes only.
10. **Tracker**: both say 5 soldiers/unarmed; consistent.
11. **Longbow**: E says 750 m (manual) vs 1000 m (guide); TRO gives "hundreds of metres" from a high platform, no figure (not decisive).
12. **Walker lineage**: TRO says Yeren is based on technology stolen from the Soviets, whereas E says Sasquatch is alien-origin and Golem is the most alien-influenced.
13. **CCA**: E says CCA units are known only by Western nicknames; TRO adds no CCA unit info and notes "CCA forces on Ganymede" lacking evidence (Chinese are the actors).
14. **Timeline**: E dates NSDF memos 1956-1959 (Arkin Jan 3 1956; Stahl July 3 1959; NSDF June 1958); TRO is June 1969 / Feb 3 1970.
15. **Ganymede/Jovian**: E's world list (Luna, Mars, Venus, Europa, Io, Titan, Achilles) has no Ganymede or Callisto; TRO adds Ganymede stats; E's Io (CCA vs US "back and forth" in guide) is consistent with TRO naming Europa, Callisto, Io as contested.
16. **Red Devil (BD-12X) and the Chinese units (Mongoose, Naga, Yeti, Emperor, Phoenix, Adder, Archer, Dragon, Yeren)** are not in E at all.
17. **Chinese Phoenix vs "Thunderbolt-equivalent"** and **Yeti vs Grizzly**: TRO equivalences only; no stats to verify.
18. ODF mappings: all UNVERIFIED. Red Devil and every Chinese unit have no known ODF; the Archer/Yeren/etc. names are not in E's tables.
